Mission "Color Detective"

Your preparation
  • Call your child over
  • Make a serious, mysterious face – like it's an important mission!
Say this to your child

"Listen carefully. There are 5 red things hidden in our kitchen. Your mission is to find them one by one and bring them quietly (like a ninja) to pile them up behind this door. Don't let the anyone hear you! Time starts now!"

Mission "Teddy Bear Fort"

Your preparation (2 min)
  • Grab 2 pillows from the couch
  • Get a blanket or throw
  • Add your child's favorite stuffed animal
  • Place everything in one spot (e.g., living room)
Say this to your child

"Teddy bear is tired and desperately needs a home where no light can get in. Your task is to build a dark cave from these things. When the cave is ready, crawl inside and check if it's completely dark. I'll come check in 15 minutes."

Mission "Inventor's Workshop"

Your preparation (10 min)
  • Find an empty cardboard box or laundry basket
  • Throw in "safe garbage": empty toilet paper rolls, egg cartons, crumpled paper
  • Add a piece of string and painter's tape (or glue stick)
  • Add a couple of markers for drawing
  • Place the box in the middle of the floor
Say this to your child

"Our spaceship broke down and we need a new robot to fix it. This box contains all the parts needed to build a super-robot. Your task is to build it. I'm starting the timer and will come see your invention in exactly 30 minutes. Happy building!"
